HOW LEARNING WORKS

PROMPT LIBRARY

Ask better questions

Use a clear prompt to tell an AI tool what you are learning and what kind of help you need.

01

Vocabulary coach

Help me practise five words about home safety. Give a simple definition and one example, then quiz me one word at a time.

02

Reading partner

Ask me three questions about this text, one at a time. After each answer, explain what I did well and give me a hint if needed.

03

Writing feedback

Review my paragraph for grammar and clarity. Point out errors, explain why, and let me try to correct them before showing a model.
